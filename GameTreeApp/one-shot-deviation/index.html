One-Shot Deviations

Subgame perfection and the one-shot deviation principle ECON 510, Sérgio O. Parreiras

Each game comes with a strategy profile s, drawn in red: at every decision node, the red edge is the action s picks there. Node colours tell the players apart (Player 1 red, Player 2 green, Player 3 blue), and each leaf lists the payoffs with Player 1's on top. Work through the questions in order; New game draws a new tree.
