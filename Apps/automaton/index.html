Strategies as Automata

Repeated games: states, outcomes, transitions ECON 510, Sérgio O. Parreiras

Build a stage game, then a strategy profile for the repeated game written as an automaton: its states, the outcome function, and the transition function. Then write down the value function, test for one-shot deviations, and check the conditions in Mathematica. Shift+Enter finishes each step.
